Your first Sunday—or any day

Come as you are.
We’ll save you a seat.

Visiting a church for the first time can feel like a big step. We want you to feel at ease, take things at your own pace and know that you’re welcome here.

What to expect

A warm welcome,
without the pressure.

Our gatherings are a time to worship, pray, hear the Bible and spend time together. You’re welcome to join in or simply take it all in. There’s no dress code and no expectation that you know the words or the routine.

01

Arrive as you are

Wear whatever you feel comfortable in. Come on your own, with friends or bring your family.

02

Take part at your pace

Sing, listen, pray or just observe. You’re free to engage in whatever way feels right to you.

03

Get to know us

We’d be glad to say hello, answer questions and help you feel at home in our community.
