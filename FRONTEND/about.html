About our church

A family learning
the way of Jesus.

Dominion Christian Gathering International is a community of people brought together by faith in Christ. We’re here to worship, grow and live out God’s love in the ordinary moments of life.

Life is better together

Who we are

Faith for the whole
of life.

We believe following Jesus shapes more than what happens inside a church building. It changes how we treat our neighbours, support one another and face the joys and challenges of each day.

So we gather around God’s word, make space for prayer and fellowship, and encourage one another to put faith into action. There is always more to learn, and you don’t need to have it all figured out to belong here.
