Why we gather

To know Christ.
To make his love known.

Our mission is to grow as followers of Jesus and to carry his hope into our homes, our neighbourhoods and the wider world.

Our commitments

What our mission
looks like in practice.

We want the heart of our church to be visible in the choices we make and the care we offer.

01

Worship with our whole lives

We gather to give thanks to God and seek his guidance. We also aim to honour him in the way we live, work and relate to others.

↗
02

Grow through God’s word

We turn to Scripture for wisdom, learn together and encourage one another to keep growing in faith.

↗
03

Welcome people with grace

We want every person to be treated with dignity, listened to with care and given room to take their next step.

↗
04

Serve with compassion

We share what we have, offer practical help and look for ways to bring encouragement to our community.
