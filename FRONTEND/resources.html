A little space to reflect

Keep growing,
one day at a time.

Faith grows through the small, steady practices of prayer, Scripture and sharing life with others. Start with this simple week of readings, at your own pace.

✳

Take a few quiet minutes. Read slowly, notice what stands out, and bring your thoughts to God.
